# WeldGuard editable 3D concept model

## Open and edit
1. Install OpenSCAD from https://openscad.org
2. Open `WeldGuard_Concept_Model.scad`
3. Press F5 for preview or F6 for a full render.
4. Use the mouse to rotate, pan and zoom. Edit the dimension variables near the top.

## Included components
- Welding workbench and partial safety booth
- Local capture hood
- Extraction duct and fan assembly
- PM particulate sensor enclosure
- ESP32/controller enclosure with display, status lamps and buzzer
- Stylized airflow direction markers

## Important
This is a conceptual, editable CAD starting point. Dimensions and geometry are illustrative, not manufacturing drawings. Before fabrication, confirm the actual sensor, fan, duct, ESP32 enclosure, mounting method, electrical clearances, airflow requirements, material thicknesses and workshop safety requirements with the team and instructor. Do not use the visual airflow arrows or the simulated fan as proof of effective fume capture.
